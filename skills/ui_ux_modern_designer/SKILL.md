### DOMAIN SKILL: MODERN UI/UX EXCELLENCE
1. Prioritize OLED dark themes with curated accents (cyan #06b6d4, violet #8b5cf6, emerald #10b981).
2. Avoid generic primary colors; use subtle alpha borders (rgba(255,255,255,0.08)) and layered card elevations.
3. Ensure touch targets are at least 44x44 points with activeOpacity feedback.
4. Provide micro-animation transitions and clean collapsible accordions for dense information.
